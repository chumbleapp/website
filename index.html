A LITTLE HELP BEFORE YOU START

Good questions.

Can’t find your answer?
Talk to the Chumble team →

How do I get the Chumble app?

Email hello@chumble.app for app availability and updates. We’ll help you find the right place to start.

Is Chumble only for food delivery?

Chumble has four services: food ordering, shopping and delivery, table booking, and event hall booking. Order a meal, shop groceries, hardware, clothing and more from local stores, reserve a restaurant table, or find a hall for your next occasion.

What can I order from stores?

Groceries, household essentials, hardware, clothing and other store items. Browse the stores and products available through Chumble to find what you need.

Who prepares the food?

The restaurant or kitchen you order from prepares your meal. Chumble connects you with restaurants and delivery partners.

How can my restaurant join?

Get in touch about becoming a restaurant partner. Tell us about your restaurant and we’ll discuss the next steps with you.

I’d like to deliver with Chumble. Where do I start?

Contact us about delivery partnerships to learn about joining the network and delivering in your community.
