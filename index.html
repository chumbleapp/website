Frequently asked
questions.

Need a hand with an order or booking?
Contact the Chumble team →

What can I do with Chumble?

You can order food, shop local stores for delivery, reserve a restaurant table or book an event hall. Contact us if you need help getting started.

How do I order food or items from a store?

Choose a restaurant or store in the app, select your meals or items, and add your delivery details. Review your order and the total cost before confirming. The restaurant prepares your food or the store supplies your items, and a delivery partner brings the order to you.

What kinds of store items can I order?

Shopping with Chumble goes beyond groceries. You can order household essentials, hardware, clothing and other products from local stores. The items you can choose from depend on the stores and stock available in the app.

How do I book a restaurant table?

Choose a restaurant, select a date and time, and reserve your table through the app. Review the reservation details before you confirm, then check your booking before heading out.

Can I book a hall for a wedding, party or meeting?

Yes. Browse the available halls for your occasion, then review the venue and booking details before confirming. If you have a question about a hall, email our team with the venue name and your planned date.

Can I track my delivery?

Yes. You can follow your order’s progress in the Chumble app. Keep your contact and delivery details accurate, and make sure someone is available to receive the order when it arrives.

How much will my order cost?

You’ll see the item prices, delivery fees and any other applicable charges before you place your order. Review the total before confirming so you know what you’re paying.

How do I get the Chumble app?

Contact us at hello@chumble.app for app availability and updates. Our team will help you get started with the service you’re interested in.

What should I do if there’s a problem with my order or booking?

Email hello@chumble.app with your order or booking details and a description of the problem. If an item is missing, incorrect or damaged, include a photo where possible so our team can understand what happened and help with the next steps.

How can my restaurant or store become a partner?

Contact us about a business partnership and tell us your business name, location and what you sell. We’ll discuss how your restaurant or store can work with Chumble and guide you through the next steps.

How do I become a delivery partner?

Get in touch about delivering with Chumble and let us know where you’d like to work. Our team will explain the requirements and how to get started.
