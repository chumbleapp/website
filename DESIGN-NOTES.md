# Chumble website reference notes

The site uses Chumble's own logo palette, locally hosted Inter typography, original copy, and independent hospitality photography. These references inform the hierarchy and customer journeys.

| Reference | Observation | Applied to Chumble |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Zomato](https://www.zomato.com/) | Food and app discovery lead the page. | Dining photograph and a clear food introduction. |
| [Bolt](https://bolt.eu/en/) | Inter typography, short service descriptions, separate partner paths. | Inter, direct headings, restaurant and courier tabs. |
| [Swiggy](https://www.swiggy.com/) | Food, groceries, and dining have separate discovery paths. | Immediate shortcuts for delivery, dining, and events. |
| [Chowdeck](https://chowdeck.com/) | Conversational opening, practical onboarding, clear vendor and rider routes. | A conversational introduction and service-specific guides. |
| [GoBites](https://www.usegobites.com/) | Ghana-focused food context, simple ordering explanation, customer FAQs. | Local-kitchen language and native, keyboard-accessible FAQs. |
| [Glovo](https://glovoapp.com/en) | Everyday delivery needs sit alongside food. | Groceries and essentials remain part of delivery copy. |
| [Just Eat](https://www.just-eat.co.uk/) | The ordering process is explained in three concise steps. | Three-step guides for each Chumble service. |
| [Deliveroo](https://deliveroo.co.uk/) | Customer actions and restaurant/courier opportunities are distinct. | Clear partner section with separate contact links. |
| [Wolt](https://wolt.com/) | Short opening copy, discovery first, approachable support. | Actionable service shortcuts and visible contact information. |

The Swiggy homepage returned a JavaScript verification screen on direct access; its indexed official homepage content was used to review its service hierarchy.

Chumble's website currently provides information and email contact links. Service shortcuts navigate to the relevant content. They do not simulate restaurant search, address coverage, live ordering, or app-store availability.

## Service structure

Four services: food ordering, shopping and delivery, table booking, and event hall booking. Shopping includes groceries, hardware, clothing, household essentials and other store products. Each service has its own shortcut, illustrated service card, and guide.

## Colour direction

The light theme uses the logo crimson (#d21c30) as the page background, white text, deeper red surfaces, and gold actions. The dark theme uses burgundy backgrounds with the same gold actions. The delivery guide retains a green section as a secondary logo colour.
